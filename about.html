IDENTITY / POSITIONALITY / REFLEXIVITY

Who I Am.

I understand hybridity not as a collection of separate roles, but as a way of living, learning, researching, teaching, and leading across intersections.

01 / MY STORY

Many ways of knowing.
One evolving self.

I am a scholar, practitioner, and multidimensional being whose experiences and commitments move across intersecting spaces. My intellectual and professional journey is grounded in curiosity about how people learn, construct identities, negotiate power, and relate to the worlds around them.

I approach knowledge as something produced not only through formal scholarship but also through embodied experience, community engagement, advocacy, and professional practice. My research and my applied work continually inform and challenge one another.

Health, fitness, and wellness are equally important to how I understand learning and agency. Through fitness education and embodied practice, I engage the body not simply as an object of study but as a site of knowledge, meaning-making, and transformation.

03 / RESEARCH

Theory. Method.
Empirical inquiry.

Theoretical

My theoretical interests include trans* of color critique, coloniality of power and gender, intersectional and transnational feminisms, and sociocultural and sociopolitical learning.

Methodological

I work across interdisciplinary, critical, feminist, queer and trans* research methods, including portraiture, autotheory, qualitative inquiry, and mixed methods.

Empirical

My research investigates trans* identity learning through bodybuilding, trans* health and well-being, learning environments, and the politics of embodiment.

05 / STUDENT SUCCESS & ADVOCACY

Learning in practice.

I have led research, strategy, and program initiatives in educational equity, student support, and professional communication. At Penn State, my work has included mixed-methods projects with university leadership and strategic planning and assessment for the R-VOICE Center. At St. George’s University, I coordinated a professional communication program supporting medical students and faculty, alongside multidisciplinary teams.

06 / LEADERSHIP & SERVICE

Responsibility as practice.

My service includes serving as Board of Directors Chair of Finance & Development for the International Association of Trans Bodybuilders and Powerlifters, Co-Editor-in-Chief of Deliberately Queer Journal, and EDI Chair for SportsHCI. I also contribute to scholarly peer review, professional associations, and institutional committees.

07 / HEALTH, FITNESS & WELLNESS

Embodied ways of knowing.

I have worked as a personal trainer, group fitness instructor, fitness educator, and coach across university and community settings. My certifications include NASM Certified Personal Trainer and AFAA Certified Group Fitness Instructor. I approach fitness and wellness as sites of learning, agency, and relational practice—not as separate from my scholarly commitments.
