My leadership philosophy

Beyond representation.

My leadership and service are grounded in a commitment to redistributing power, expanding representation, and cultivating communities where diverse identities, experiences, and ways of knowing are recognized and valued. I understand leadership as a relational and equity-centered practice that extends beyond positional authority to encompass advocacy, accountability, and collective transformation.

Across scholarly publishing, nonprofit governance, professional associations, and mentorship, I work to challenge exclusionary structures, advance underrepresented perspectives, and create opportunities for meaningful participation. These commitments are inseparable from my scholarly praxis, informing how I navigate institutional power, cultivate intergenerational relationships, and contribute to communities of learning and practice.
