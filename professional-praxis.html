01 / Featured initiatives

Research-informed institutional change.

My work at Penn State has involved collaborating across institutional units and leadership groups to examine educational equity, strengthen assessment, and support evidence-informed planning.

Educational equity leadership

University Equity Leadership Conversations (UELC)

Led a grounded theory project engaging senior leadership across more than 15 Penn State colleges. Interviews and focus groups explored how leaders navigate institutional change, educational equity, and practices supporting student belonging and success.

Office of the Vice Provost for Educational Equity · Penn State
Assessment and analytics

Bias Response Network (BRN)

Led a longitudinal analysis of eight years of bias-response data, collaborating with the Bias Response Network and institutional analytics partners toward the development of an inaugural Power BI dashboard.
